LifeThreads 1.0 – Notes for App Review

Build 24 · GK Coding · george.kassih@gkcoding.dev

What changed since the last submission (build 23)

GuidelineChange
1.2 User-Generated ContentFamily walls now filter objectionable words on the device before anything is posted: memory title, story and place, the wall's name and the member's display name, in English, German and Arabic. A blocked post shows the message “This has words that aren't allowed on family walls. Please change the text and try again.” and nothing is sent. Family walls are end-to-end encrypted, so the check runs on the device before encryption.
3.1.2 SubscriptionsEvery App Store description (English, German, Arabic) ends with a working link to the Terms of Use (EULA) and the Privacy Policy. German and Arabic link their own translated pages.
—The Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links in the app (Settings, Premium page, family wall rules) open in the app's language.

How to test the filter

  1. On the wall tap ⋯ › Family walls and sign in with Apple.
  2. Accept the family wall rules and create a wall (needs a Sandbox subscription: Settings › Premium).
  3. Tap Add memory and type an offensive word as the title, then save. The app shows the message above and nothing is posted.
  4. The same check applies to the wall's name (rename) and to your display name.

Safeguards for family walls (guideline 1.2)

RequirementIn LifeThreads
Terms with zero toleranceBefore using family walls, everyone accepts the family wall rules (zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users), linked to the Terms of Use: gkcoding.dev/lifethreads/terms
FilteringOn-device word filter before posting (new in build 24).
ReportingAny memory or member can be reported from the wall. Reports reach GK Coding and are reviewed within 24 hours; violating content is removed and the account suspended or deleted.
BlockingAny member can be blocked; their memories are hidden. The owner can remove members and their memories.
Contactinfo@gkcoding.dev, published on gkcoding.dev/lifethreads and in the app.

Features and where to find them (guideline 2.5.19)

There are no hidden, dormant or undocumented features. Everything in the app:

  • Memory wall, timeline and map; add, connect and arrange memories (free up to 30 memories).
  • Premium (lifethreads_premium_yearly, lifethreads_premium_monthly): unlimited memories, more walls, wall themes, Create Story with music, Thread of the Year, On this day, family walls. Settings › Premium shows price, renewal terms, Terms/Privacy links and Restore.
  • Family walls: ⋯ › Family walls. Account deletion: ⋯ › Family walls › Account › Delete account.
  • Home screen widget: Settings › Home screen widget.
  • App lock with Face ID: Settings › Privacy. Hidden memories: View › Hidden memories (after Face ID).
  • Free full backup: Settings › Backup & data.
  • Show the wall on another screen: ⋯ › Display wall.
  • Music in Create Story is CC0 (HoliznaCC0, Komiku): Settings › Music credits.

No sign-in is needed for the app itself: on first launch choose “Preview demo wall” to see nine sample memories.
