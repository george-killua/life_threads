## English

**I built an app for the moments you don't want to forget. On Saturday it opens for testing. 🧵**

For the past months I've been building **LifeThreads**: a private memory wall for your phone.

You pin your moments to a wall — the photos, the place, who was there and how it felt — and
connect them with threads: your first home, the day your child was born, the summer by the sea.
Then you can turn them into a story or a short film with music, ready for Instagram, WhatsApp or
Snapchat, or look back at your year in memories.

What matters most to me: **your memories stay on your phone.** No account needed, no cloud, no ads.
You decide what you share.

👉 **Testing starts this Saturday, 17 October**, on iPhone and Android.

Want to be one of the first? **Send me an email at info@gkcoding.dev** with
"LifeThreads tester" and whether you use iPhone or Android.

🎁 Everyone who tests early gets **2 months of LifeThreads Premium for free.**

Honest feedback is the best gift you can give a solo developer. Thank you! 🙏
