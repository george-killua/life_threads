sources in `assets/music/LICENSES.md`: from this list 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 (Musopen / CC0 recordings

"The Death of Åse" and "Morning Mood" (Musopen), and Komiku's CC0 "Dreaming of you" and "Sad walk

## What a track must allow

A bundled track is copied inside a commercial app and ends up in videos that users post on
Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, … without any credit line in the video. So the licence must allow,
without conditions we cannot meet:

1. redistribution inside an app (the file ships in the app bundle),
2. commercial use (the app is sold / has Premium),
3. use in derivative works made and published by end users,
4. no attribution inside every video (we can credit in Settings → Music credits, but users'
   videos carry no credits).

That leaves **CC0 1.0** and **public domain** (composition *and* recording). CC BY (e.g. Kevin
MacLeod / incompetech, Scott Buckley) is avoided because rule 4 cannot be guaranteed for videos
users publish.

### Why not Pixabay

The Pixabay Content License forbids selling or distributing content "on a Standalone basis",
i.e. without creative effort applied
([licence summary](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/)). A music library inside the app
that users pick songs from is close to redistributing the tracks as they are, and Pixabay's own
terms (not the summary) decide the edge cases. The plan already rules it out; this list contains
no Pixabay track.

### Why not FreePD

FreePD.com (Kevin MacLeod's public-domain site, the source suggested in the plan) says it has
been taken offline and is permanently closed (checked 2026-10-12), so its tracks can no longer be
traced to a live source page with licence terms.

### Classical recordings

A composition by a composer who died long ago is public domain, but the **recording** has its own
rights. Only recordings whose performers released them (Musopen's "Set Chopin Free" and Kickstarter
projects, and Musopen uploads marked CC0 / public domain on Wikimedia Commons) are listed. Musopen
*asks* for in-line credit as a courtesy; the release itself has no conditions. The in-app credits
cover it. Long pieces are trimmed to a ~30 s excerpt (allowed: public domain / CC0 permit edits).

## Open points for the owner

- Classical excerpts sound "classical"; if the app should feel more contemporary, the modern
  CC0 pool is thin (mostly game music on OpenGameArt). An alternative is commissioning a few
  short pieces (work for hire, full rights) — the cleanest licence of all.
- Before bundling, listen to every file for quality (some Commons recordings are older uploads)
  and save each licence page (PDF or HTML) to `assets/music/licenses/`.
- Musopen requests in-line credit: the Settings → Music credits list names performer and source.
