Video brief · LifeThreads app

Three short videos for TikTok, Reels and Shorts

LifeThreads turns your photos into a private wall of memories, tied together with gold threads. We need three vertical videos that show real, happy people and the real app. One shoot day, shot on an iPhone, is enough.

Shoot plan

Locations (2)

  • Outdoors at golden hour: a park, lake or beach. Friends and a picnic.
  • A warm home: living room with a sofa, a dining table, ideally a garden or balcony.

People (4–6)

  • A group of 3–4 friends in their 20s–30s
  • A couple
  • A parent with a child (for the birthday shot)
  • One person for V2 who is comfortable talking to camera

Props

  • Birthday cake with candles, a few balloons
  • Picnic blanket, drinks, fairy lights for the evening
  • Two phones: one iPhone that runs LifeThreads, one for selfies

Look

  • Bright, warm light, real laughter, nothing staged-looking
  • Handheld is fine; keep it steady on the phone screens
  • The app is dark with gold; the people shots are warm and sunny

Filming the app

  • Always show the real app. Never redraw or fake a screen.
  • George sets up the phone with the demo memories and photos before the shoot (⋯ › Tutorial loads them).
  • For crisp screen shots, use the iPhone’s own screen recording (Control Center) and cut it in. Film the phone in a hand for the “real life” feel.
  • Before filming: Do Not Disturb on, battery charged, brightness at maximum, no notifications.
  • V1 and V2 are needed in English and in German: switch the app language in Settings › Language, and record the screens again.

Text safe zone

TikTok and Instagram put their buttons and captions over the video. Keep all captions and the logo out of the hatched areas: the top ~250 px, the bottom ~400 px and the right ~130 px of the 1080×1920 frame.

Rules

Must

  • A signed model release from everyone on camera, and from a parent for every child
  • Permission to film at the locations if needed
  • Music licensed for commercial and ad use on social media (for example from a stock music library). Trending TikTok sounds can’t be used in ads.

Avoid

  • Other brand logos on clothes, drinks or screens
  • Years or dates in captions
  • Showing real private messages or personal photos of people who didn’t agree

Delivery

  • For each video: a version with captions burned in, and a clean version without text
  • V1 and V2 in English and German, with separate music and voice tracks if possible
  • All raw footage handed over at the end
  • First cut within one week of the shoot, then one round of changes, then final files
  • GK Coding may use all videos and footage without time limit on every channel, including paid ads
