Format: 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, 20 seconds. English.
Style: warm, cinematic, evening light; colours dark charcoal (#0A070B) and soft gold; calm piano or acoustic music, no drums.
Rules: real people in the human shots, natural and unposed. No years, dates or "2025" in any caption. No brand names other than LifeThreads.

## Prompt to paste into Grok

> Create a 20-second vertical (9:16) ad for the app LifeThreads, in English. Warm, cinematic, evening light, dark charcoal and soft gold colours, calm piano music. Alternate real human moments with the attached app screen recording, using these cuts:
> 0–3 s a young mother at night scrolling endless photos on her phone, a little lost – caption "Thousands of photos."
> 3–6 s app footage 1.0–4.0 s (the photo wall) – "Pin the ones that matter to one wall."
> 6–9 s a couple slow-dancing at their wedding under string lights, then app footage 7.5–10.5 s – "Keep the story behind every photo."
> 9–12 s app footage 18.0–21.0 s (the story film) – "Turn memories into a film with music."
> 12–15 s a grandmother and grandchild smiling at a phone together, then app footage 26.8–29.8 s – "One wall for the whole family."
> 15–17 s app footage 32.3–34.3 s – "Save a moment in seconds."
> 17–20 s end card on dark charcoal with the LifeThreads icon: "LifeThreads – Your life, threaded together. Free on the App Store & Google Play."
> No years or dates in captions. Keep the app footage unchanged (don't redraw the screens).

## Note
The memory screen shows that memory's own date ("Salzburg • Jun 17, 2023"). That is when the wedding happened, a memory from the past, so it does not date the ad the way a "2025" recap title would. If you'd rather show no date at all, ask Grok to cover that line with the caption, or skip this clip and hold the wall shot longer.
