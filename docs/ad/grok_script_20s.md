Format: 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, 20 seconds. English.
Mood: **happy and full of life.** Real people laughing, taking photos of their best moments and
saving them in the app. Bright daylight and golden-hour sun, warm colours. The app screens stay
dark with gold (#0A070B and gold).
Music: upbeat feel-good indie pop with acoustic guitar, hand claps and whistling, about 115 BPM.
Light camera-shutter sounds on every photo. No sad piano.
Voice-over: a friendly, energetic, smiling voice, speaking like it's talking to a friend.
Rules: no years or dates in captions; no brand names except LifeThreads.
