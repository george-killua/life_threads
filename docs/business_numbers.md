# LifeThreads – Costs, Revenue and Profit (estimate)

Rough planning numbers from 15 October 2026. **Check current prices and ask
a tax advisor** – these are not tax or financial advice.

## Assumptions

- Seller in Austria: prices include 20 % VAT.
- Apple App Store Small Business Program and Google Play's 15 % rate
  (otherwise 30 %).
- Sync: about 1 GB per Premium user (≈ 300 memories × 2 photos × 1.5 MB).
- Object storage: €0.006–0.02 per GB per month.

## What one subscriber brings (after VAT and store fee)

| | Yearly €34.99 | Monthly €4.99 |
|---|---|---|
| Price incl. VAT | €34.99 | €59.88 per year |
| minus 20 % VAT | €29.16 | €49.90 |
| minus 15 % store fee | **≈ €24.80 / year** | **≈ €42.40 / year** |

## Costs

| Per user (sync storage) | €0.07–0.25 per year |
|---|---|
| VPS (already running) | ~€60–180 per year |
| Apple Developer Program | ~€99 per year |
| Domain, email, small things | ~€30 per year |
| **Fixed total** | **≈ €200–300 per year** |

## Scenarios (yearly subscribers, before income tax)

| Paying users | Revenue | Costs | Profit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | €300 | ~€300 | €0 (break-even) |
| 100 | €2,480 | ~€320 | ≈ €2,150 |
| 1,000 | €24,800 | ~€550 | ≈ €24,000 |
| 5,000 | €124,000 | ~€1,500 | ≈ €122,000 |

## What matters

- Storage is under 1 % of revenue per user – sync does not threaten profit.
- Paid apps convert about 2–5 % of active users. 1,000 paying users need
  roughly 20,000–50,000 active users: marketing and retention decide the
  result, not servers.
- Income tax comes off the profit; refunds and cancellations lower it a
  little.
