# Story music licences

Every song in `assets/music/` and in `lib/features/stories/data/music_catalog.dart` has a row
here. A track may only be bundled when its licence clearly allows shipping it inside a
commercial app and using it in videos that users make and share (CC0 preferred). Save a copy
of the licence page as it was on the download date in `assets/music/licenses/` (not bundled in
the app) and link it in the last column.
