# LifeThreads Talking Ad Plan

## Best Free Route

Use Runway Lip Sync first.

- Free plan can include 125 credits.
- Lip Sync costs 5 credits per second.
- Target output: 20-24 seconds.
- Format: vertical 9:16.
- Use two realistic people, forward-facing, shoulders-up when speaking.

Backup free route: HeyGen Free.

- 3 videos/month.
- Better for one presenter/avatar.
- Less good for two people naturally talking to each other.

Google Flow/Veo is best for cinematic dialogue, but it is not the free route.

## Short Ad Script

Scene: Two friends/family members just took a photo together. One person holds the phone.

Person A:
"This photo is nice, but I always forget the story behind it."

Person B:
"Save it in LifeThreads. Add the people, place, and what happened."

Person A:
"So it becomes more than a photo?"

Person B:
"Exactly. It becomes part of your memory wall."

Person A:
"Now I can find it again, and see what it connects to."

End card:
"LifeThreads. Save what matters."

## Runway Visual Prompt

Create a photorealistic vertical 9:16 smartphone-style video or image for lip sync.
Two adults in a warm modern home at evening, sitting close together at a table, looking natural and friendly.
One person holds a smartphone after taking a memory photo. Both faces are clearly visible, forward-facing, shoulders-up framing, not too close together.
Natural indoor lighting, realistic skin texture, subtle handheld camera feel, no text, no logos, no watermark, no exaggerated expressions.
The mood is personal, warm, honest, and app-advertisement ready.
